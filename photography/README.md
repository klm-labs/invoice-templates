# Photography invoice template

A photography invoice separates your time (the session fee) from what you deliver (final images, prints), and says in plain words who may use the photos and how. The filled-in sample below does both.

Web page: [https://klmlabs.app/invoice-maker/invoice-templates/photography/](https://klmlabs.app/invoice-maker/invoice-templates/photography/)  
Filled-in sample PDF (US Letter): [photography.pdf](./photography.pdf)  
Line items as data: [line-items.csv](./line-items.csv)

## The sample invoice

- INV-218, issued Oct 27, 2026, due Nov 10, 2026 (net 14)
- From Maren Holt Photography to Brightwater Dental Group (made-up business and client)

| # | Description | Qty | Unit price | Amount |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Team headshot session on location, half day (session fee) | 1 | $850.00 | $850.00 |
| 2 | Retouched headshots, final edits | 14 | $45.00 | $630.00 |
| 3 | Rush delivery, 48 hours | 1 | $150.00 | $150.00 |
| 4 | 8x10 prints, luster | 4 | $25.00 | $100.00 |
| 5 | Travel, 38 miles round trip | 38 | $0.70 | $26.60 |

Subtotal: **$1,756.60**  
Total: **$1,756.60**

Each amount is quantity × unit price, rounded half up to the cent; tax, where there is any, is worked on the subtotal the same way. These are the Invoice Maker app's own money rules.

## Notes printed on the invoice

> Usage license: Brightwater Dental Group may use these images on its website, social media and printed marketing, with no time limit. Resale, stock use or use by anyone else needs written permission. Maren Holt Photography keeps the copyright.  
> Gallery link and download codes were sent on Oct 26.  

## Why the lines are built this way

- The session fee is separate from the images, so the client can see what the time cost and what each final edit cost.
- Final images are counted (14 at $45), which answers the usual question of how many edits were included.
- Rush delivery and prints are their own lines; leave them out when they don't apply.
- The usage license goes in the notes: it says who may use the photos, where and for how long. This is the line clients ask about most later.
